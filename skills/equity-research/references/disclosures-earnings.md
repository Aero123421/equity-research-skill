## 1. Change log 2024–2026 (as of 2026-10; verify when it matters)

**Japan**
- **四半期報告書 abolished** for quarters starting on/after 2024-04-01. Now:
  有価証券報告書 (annual), **半期報告書** (H1, within 45 days, reviewed),
  and Q1/Q3 **決算短信 only**. Q1/Q3 短信 now include segment information and
  cash-flow notes; CPA review is optional unless specific conditions apply
  (e.g. a prior non-clean opinion, material weakness, late filing). A Q1/Q3
  短信 marked "レビュー: 無" is normal.
- **TSE cost-of-capital request** (2023, updated repeatedly; April 2026
  update centered on appropriate allocation of management resources). TSE
  publishes a monthly list of companies' disclosure status.
- **Pre-AGM 有報 filing** encouraged by the FSA (2025) and the July 2026 CG
  Code revision (ideally 3+ weeks before the AGM).
- **Prime market English disclosure** of results and timely disclosures is
  mandatory (grace period ended 2026-03-31).
- **Sustainability disclosure (SSBJ)** phased in by market cap: ≥¥3tn from FY
  ending March 2027, then smaller tiers later; assurance a year after.
- **Corporate Governance Code revised** July 2026; CG reports reflecting it
  due by end of July 2027.
- **TOB and large-holding rules** revised effective 2026-05-01 (see
  `events-catalysts.md`).
- **J-GAAP goodwill amortization retained** (2026 decision).
- **New lease standard** (lessee on-balance-sheet) applies from FY beginning
  on/after 2027-04-01 — a comparability break for EBITDA, operating CF and
  leverage.
- 特設注意市場銘柄 is now **特別注意銘柄**.

**US / global**
- **Optional semiannual reporting (Form 10-S)** proposed May 2026; not
  adopted as of Oct 2026. Assume quarterly 10-Qs unless a final rule exists
  and the company elected it.
- **ASU 2023-07** (segment expenses provided to the CODM) in effect.
- **ASU 2024-03** expense disaggregation: annual periods beginning after
  2026-12-15.
- **Cybersecurity:** 10-K Item 1C and 8-K Item 1.05.
- **Non-GAAP:** SEC's December 2022 interpretations (misleading adjustments,
  tailored accounting, labels, prominence).
- **IFRS 18** (operating profit subtotal, management-defined performance
  measures) effective 2027-01-01 — affects Japanese and other IFRS filers.

## 2. Japan documents

Two regimes: exchange rules (TSE: 決算短信, 適時開示, CG報告書, via TDnet)
and statute (FIEA: 有価証券報告書, 半期報告書, 臨時報告書, 訂正報告書,
大量保有報告書, via EDINET). The free TDnet viewer keeps only about a month;
older disclosures are on company IR pages and the TSE listed-company search.

### 決算短信 (earnings summary; unaudited)
- **Timing:** promptly after results are determined (≈45 days max; 30 days
  desirable). March FY: full year early–mid May; Q1 early Aug; Q2 late Oct–
  mid Nov; Q3 early Feb. Often after the 15:00/15:30 close; PTS and next open
  react.
- **Summary page:** 経営成績 (売上高, 営業利益, 経常利益, 当期純利益, EPS,
  YoY), 財政状態 (自己資本比率, BPS), cash flows (annual), 配当の状況 (incl.
  配当性向, DOE), next-year 業績予想, notes on scope of consolidation changes,
  accounting policy changes/restatements, and share counts (issued incl.
  treasury, treasury, average).
- **Pitfalls:**
  - Quarterly figures are **cumulative**; derive single quarters (Q2 = 6M −
    3M; Q4 = FY − 9M).
  - 会計方針の変更 / 修正再表示 change the comparison base.
  - 連結範囲の変更 means growth is not organic.
  - Use shares outstanding ex-treasury for per-share and market-cap math.
  - IFRS/US GAAP filers use different line items (no 経常利益).

### 業績予想 and revisions
- Company forecasts are expected but voluntary; some give ranges or 未定.
- **Revision disclosure rule** (consolidated): required when the new forecast
  (or the actual at results time) vs the latest published forecast is
  **sales ≥1.1× or ≤0.9×**, or **operating profit, ordinary profit or net
  profit ≥1.3× or ≤0.7×** (any one line). Sign changes also trigger.
  Standalone figures add net-asset tests.
- **Dividend forecast revisions have no threshold** — any change must be
  disclosed.
- **Pitfalls:** a forecast that wasn't revised can still be up to ~25–30% off;
  "修正の有無: 無" means the published number didn't change, not that results
  are on track; a 業績予想の修正 or 差異 notice can come days before the 短信;
  initial forecasts are conservative at many companies — test with the
  company's own history.

### 進捗率 (progress rate)
進捗率 = cumulative actual ÷ full-year forecast. Compare against the company's
**own historical** progress at the same quarter (3–5 years), not 25/50/75%.
Seasonal companies (construction, real estate, retail, year-end-heavy
software/systems integrators) distort; Q4 charges can absorb an apparent
surplus; revisions reset the denominator.

### 決算説明資料 / 説明会 (IR presentation; voluntary)
Read: operating profit bridge (price/volume/mix, materials, FX, SG&A,
one-offs), segment vs forecast, **想定為替レート and per-¥1 sensitivity**,
revision reasons, shareholder returns, capex/R&D plans, mid-term plan
progress, Q&A summaries. **Pitfalls:** company-defined profit measures
(コア営業利益, 事業利益, 調整後) differ from 短信 営業利益; restated segment
history; "実質" or FX-excluded growth hiding reported declines. Tie at least
one slide figure back to the 短信.

### 有価証券報告書 (annual report; audited; within 3 months of FYE)
| Section | Read for | Pitfalls |
|---|---|---|
| 主要な経営指標等の推移 | 5-year trend | Restatements |
| 事業の内容, 関係会社の状況 | Business model, parent/subsidiaries (親子上場), affiliates | Listed subsidiaries' minorities |
| 経営方針・対処すべき課題 | Strategy, KPIs | Aspirational language |
| 事業等のリスク | Risks with likelihood/impact/countermeasures; **diff vs prior year** | Boilerplate; risks dropped after materializing |
| MD&A (経営者による分析) | Drivers, liquidity, **重要な会計上の見積り** (impairment, DTA, inventory) | Explains what, rarely why |
| セグメント情報 / 関連情報 | Segment profit, assets, capex; **主要な顧客ごとの情報 (customers ≥10% of sales)** | Segment profit basis ≠ consolidated OP; customers sometimes anonymized |
| 収益認識注記 | Contract assets/liabilities, 残存履行義務 | Thinner than US RPO |
| 設備の状況 | Planned capex (budget, timing, funding) | Plans slip; compare with actual |
| 大株主の状況 | Top 10 holders at FYE | 信託口 (custody trusts) are not economic owners; use 大量保有報告書 for real holders |
| 役員の状況, 報酬 | Bios, outside directors, pay (≥¥100m individual disclosure) | Board activity and attendance |
| 監査の状況 | Auditor, tenure, fees | Auditor change; small-firm switch |
| **株式の保有状況 (政策保有株式)** | Cross-holdings: count, amounts, purpose, reduction policy | Sale gains as one-offs; large holdings vs own market cap |
| 従業員の状況 | Headcount, pay, human-capital metrics | Parent-only vs consolidated |
| 経理の状況 (notes) | Financial instruments, pensions, tax effect, contingencies, related parties, subsequent events, borrowings schedule | Off-balance items |
| 監査報告書 / KAM, 内部統制報告書 | Opinion; KAM topics; material weakness (開示すべき重要な不備) | A **new** KAM = new risk |

### 半期報告書
Within 45 days of H1 end; reviewed, not audited; filed after the Q2 短信.

### 適時開示 (TDnet) — signals worth flagging
決算発表延期, 有報提出期限延長, 第三者委員会の設置, 過年度決算訂正, 特別損失の
計上, 行使価額修正条項付新株予約権 (MS warrants), 親会社による TOB, 上場維持
基準 non-compliance, 監査法人の異動, 継続企業の前提に関する注記. Titles are
often neutral ("〜に関するお知らせ") — read the first paragraph; "差異" in the
title signals a gap vs forecast.

### Other Japanese documents
- **月次売上/月次KPI** (voluntary): definitions vary (既存店 usually ≥13
  months); calendar and price effects; sudden discontinuation is a red flag.
- **中期経営計画**: check the previous plan's achievement, redefined/lowered
  targets, cash allocation, links to executive pay; rolling plans never
  arrive.
- **統合報告書**: strategy and governance color; not a source for financial
  truth.
- **コーポレートガバナンス報告書**: 支配株主, independent directors,
  comply-or-explain, cross-holding policy, takeover defense, minority
  protection, **資本コストや株価を意識した経営** status (initial / update /
  under consideration).
- **株主総会招集通知** and voting results (臨時報告書 after the AGM): low
  approval for the top executive signals dissent.
- **臨時報告書**: AGM results, change of parent/major shareholder, auditor
  change (with reasons), large offerings, governance/shareholder agreements.
- **訂正報告書**: amount, years, cause, internal-control impact, third-party
  committee report.
- **継続企業の前提に関する注記**: 重要事象等 vs material uncertainty;
  liquidity runway, lender waivers.
- **監理銘柄 (確認中/審査中) / 整理銘柄 / 特別注意銘柄**: a normal delisting
  after a TOB also goes through 整理銘柄 — check the reason.
- **大量保有報告書 / 変更報告書, 自己株券買付状況報告書, 公開買付届出書,
  意見表明報告書**.
- Japanese companies with US listings file 20-F/6-K; from 2026 their
  directors/officers may file Section 16 reports.

## 3. US documents

### 10-K (annual; audited)
| Item | Read for |
|---|---|
| 1 Business | Segments, customers/concentration, competition, regulation, backlog, seasonality |
| 1A Risk Factors | **Diff vs prior year**; risks that "have occurred" vs "could" |
| 1B Unresolved staff comments | Rare and meaningful |
| 1C Cybersecurity | Governance; check for Item 1.05 8-Ks |
| 3 Legal proceedings | Cross-check contingencies note; "not estimable" amounts |
| 5 Market / repurchases | Monthly buybacks; net of SBC |
| 7 MD&A | Drivers, liquidity and material cash requirements, critical accounting estimates, KPI definitions and changes |
| 7A Market risk | Rate/FX/commodity sensitivity |
| 8 Financial statements | Auditor report and **CAMs**; notes on segments (ASU 2023-07 expenses), revenue/RPO, leases, debt, SBC, taxes, contingencies, subsequent events |
| 9 / 9A | Auditor changes/disagreements; **material weaknesses** |
| 9B | Director/officer 10b5-1 plan adoptions/terminations |
| 15 Exhibits | Credit agreements (covenants), material contracts, subsidiaries |
Deadlines: 60/75/90 days after FYE by filer size. 52/53-week years and
recast segments need care.

### 10-Q (Q1–Q3; reviewed)
Q4 = FY − 9M YTD. Part II: legal, risk factor updates, repurchases, 10b5-1
plan disclosures. Deadlines 40/45 days.

### 8-K items worth monitoring (4 business days)
| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2.02 | Earnings release (furnished); non-GAAP reconciliations; numbers can change in the 10-Q |
| 1.01 / 1.02 | Material agreements (credit amendments, covenant waivers, big contracts, M&A) |
| 1.05 | Material cybersecurity incident (4 business days after materiality determination) |
| 2.05 / 2.06 | Restructuring costs / material impairments |
| 2.03 / 2.04 | New obligations / acceleration triggers |
| 3.01 / 3.02 | Listing-standard notices / unregistered equity sales |
| **4.01** | Auditor change (reasons, disagreements, Ex. 16 letter) |
| **4.02** | Non-reliance on prior financials — the most severe restatement signal |
| 5.02 | Officer/director departures and appointments (sudden CFO exits) |
| 5.07 | Shareholder vote results |
| 7.01 / 8.01 | Reg FD materials / other events (buyback authorizations, litigation) |

### Earnings releases, guidance, calls
Guidance is voluntary: range width, change vs prior, assumptions (FX, tax,
share count), GAAP vs non-GAAP basis. Calls: prepared remarks are scripted;
Q&A shows analyst focus and evasions. Vendor transcripts can contain errors.

### Other US documents
- **DEF 14A:** pay metrics (often non-GAAP), pay-vs-performance, equity plan
  share reserve (future dilution), related-party transactions, ownership.
- **S-1/S-3/S-8/424B:** IPO details and lock-ups, shelf capacity, ATM
  programs, convert terms, employee plan dilution.
- **NT 10-K / NT 10-Q:** late filing; often precedes restatements, auditor
  resignations or going-concern issues.
- **SEC comment letters (UPLOAD/CORRESP):** released ≥20 business days after
  review completion; non-GAAP, segments, revenue recognition, KPIs.
- **XBRL company facts:** the same period appears in multiple filings;
  derive Q4; custom tags for KPIs; de-duplicate by latest filing; not
  split-adjusted.

### Non-GAAP rules (Reg G / Item 10(e))
Most comparable GAAP measure with equal or greater prominence;
reconciliation; no misleading labels; excluding normal recurring cash
operating expenses can be misleading; tailored accounting (e.g. accelerating
revenue) prohibited.

## 4. Earnings analysis checklist

### 4.1 Four baselines
1. Actual vs **company forecast/guidance** (initial and latest).
2. Actual vs **consensus** — confirm the basis (US adjusted EPS is usually
   non-GAAP; Japan consensus often sits above conservative company
   forecasts; small caps may have few or no analysts).
3. vs **prior year** (restated) and **prior quarter** (seasonality).
   Calendarize when comparing March-FY Japanese companies with December-FY US
   peers.
4. vs the stock's **price reaction** — what the market expected.
A beat driven by tax rate, share count or one-offs is low quality.

### 4.2 Guidance changes
Japan: thresholds above; read stated reasons and the implied Q4 (or H2).
US: raised / lowered / narrowed / withdrawn; note sandbagging patterns.

### 4.3 Segment mix
Identify the profit pool; corporate cost allocation; re-orgs; growth
excluding M&A and FX; Japan 主要な顧客 ≥10%; US segment expenses.

### 4.4 Margin bridge
Volume, price, mix, input costs, FX, wages/SG&A, R&D, one-offs. Gross margin
definitions differ (D&A in COGS or not; R&D placement in Japan).

### 4.5 Revenue quality
DSO vs growth; contract assets vs contract liabilities; deferred revenue;
RPO/残存履行義務; bill-and-hold; extended terms; gross vs net; channel
inventory; quarter-end loading; customer concentration; related-party sales.
Receivables growing faster than revenue for two quarters is an early warning.

### 4.6 Inventory
Days inventory vs sales; finished goods vs raw materials; write-downs.
Rising inventory + falling margin = demand/pricing problem.

### 4.7 Capex, FCF, working capital
Capex ÷ D&A; planned vs actual capex; capitalized development/software.
FCF definition must be stated (Japanese sources often use operating +
investing CF, which includes securities and M&A). FCF conversion = FCF ÷ net
income. Watch factoring and supplier finance inflating operating CF; lease
principal in financing CF under IFRS 16/ASC 842 (and Japan from 2027).

### 4.8 Share count, dilution, SBC
Basic vs diluted, period-end vs average; converts and 新株予約権; MS
warrants (toxic dilution); third-party allotments; treasury cancellations;
net buyback = repurchases − issuance; SBC ÷ revenue and ÷ FCF.

### 4.9 Non-GAAP reconciliation
Start from GAAP; check each add-back, tax effects, consistency, changed
definitions; track the gap between adjusted and GAAP growth.

### 4.10 Tax rate
Effective vs statutory (Japan ≈30%); valuation allowance / 繰延税金資産
changes swing Japanese net income; normalize.

### 4.11 FX
Japan: 想定為替レート vs actual average; per-¥1 sensitivity; translation vs
transaction; hedging; 為替差損益 in non-operating. US: constant-currency
growth, 7A sensitivity. A weaker yen lifts exporters' reported profit without
operational improvement; next year's guidance will reset the rate.

### 4.12 Restatements, audit and going concern
Japan: 修正再表示 notes, 訂正報告書; opinion types (無限定適正, 限定付適正,
不適正, 意見不表明); adverse/disclaimer triggers TSE measures. US: 4.02 ("Big
R") vs revision ("little r"); going-concern paragraph; CAMs; ICFR opinions;
auditor fees and tenure.

## 5. Accounting-basis differences

| Topic | J-GAAP | IFRS | US GAAP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating profit | 売上総利益 − 販管費; non-operating items below; 経常利益 exists | Not defined until IFRS 18 (2027); companies choose what's included (some include other income/expenses, equity-method income) | Income from operations; no ordinary profit |
| Extraordinary items | 特別損益 below 経常利益 | None | None |
| Goodwill | **Amortized** (up to 20 years) — lowers OP vs IFRS/US peers | Not amortized; impairment test | Not amortized (public companies) |
| Impairment reversal | Prohibited | Allowed (except goodwill) | Prohibited |
| Gains on cross-holding sales | 特別利益 / net income | FVOCI: no recycling to P&L | Through net income |
| Leases | Operating leases off-balance until new standard (FY from 2027-04) | On-balance (IFRS 16) | On-balance (ASC 842) |
| LIFO | Not allowed | Not allowed | Allowed |
Japanese IFRS adopters' 営業利益 may include impairments and gains that
J-GAAP places below 経常利益. Build an adjusted operating profit before
comparing peers.

## 6. Earnings-quality red flags and next checks

| Flag | Check next |
|---|---|
| Receivables/contract assets growing faster than sales | Revenue note, concentration, Q4 share of sales, subsequent collection |
| Operating CF persistently below net income (high accruals) | CF lines, capitalized costs, factoring, one-offs |
| Inventory up while sales flat or margin falling | Inventory aging, write-down policy, backlog |
| Gross margin up without a cost story; capitalized costs rising | Capitalized development, useful-life changes |
| Widening adjusted-vs-GAAP gap; new add-backs | Reconciliation, comment letters |
| "One-time" charges every year | 5-year history of restructuring/特別損失 |
| Net profit driven by 特別利益 or DTA recognition | Core OP, tax note, cross-holding disclosures |
| Repeated forecast cuts; forecast withheld (未定) | Revision history; initial vs actual track record |
| Results delay, filing extension, NT 10-K/10-Q | Reason, auditor statements, follow-up filings |
| Auditor change (mid-year, to smaller firm, disagreements) | 臨時報告書 / 8-K 4.01, fee trend |
| Restatement, 特別注意銘柄, material weakness | Periods, amounts, root cause, committee report |
| Going-concern note / 重要事象等 | Liquidity, covenants, waivers, raise terms |
| MS warrants / MSCB / large third-party allotment / ATM | Terms, investor identity, use of proceeds |
| CFO/CEO departures near results | 8-K 5.02 / 役員異動, successor |
| Large goodwill vs equity; early impairment | Impairment assumptions, KAM/CAM |
| Segment re-org without recast | Recast history |
| Customer concentration, related-party sales | 主要な顧客, related-party note |
| Shareholder returns exceeding FCF; debt-funded returns | FCF, net debt, covenants |
| Large tax-rate swings | Tax reconciliation |
| FX windfall driving growth | 想定為替 vs actual, sensitivity |
| Low AGM approval, activist filings | 招集通知, vote results, 13D/大量保有 |
| Insider selling clusters; 10b5-1 changes near results | Form 4, 10-K Item 9B |
| New KAM/CAM on revenue or valuation | Audit procedures, estimates note |
| Monthly KPI disclosure suddenly stops | IR page history |
| Cash-flow classification changes; supplier finance growth | Financing arrangement notes, payables days |

## 7. Capital policy and governance checks (lens L5)

- **Returns:** dividend policy (payout, DOE, progressive), buyback authorized
  vs executed, total payout ratio, funding (FCF vs debt), cancellation of
  treasury shares.
- **Balance sheet:** net cash/debt, cash vs market cap, investment securities
  and cross-holdings at market value, pension deficits, leases.
- **Cost of capital (Japan):** has the company disclosed its analysis and
  targets (ROE, ROIC, P/B, cost of equity)? Status on TSE's list; credibility
  vs track record.
- **Ownership structure:** parent company and listed subsidiaries, founder
  control, cross-holders, activists, foreign ownership.
- **Dilution sources:** SBC, converts, warrants, ATMs, equity plans.
- **Governance:** board independence, related-party transactions, takeover
  defenses, AGM voting, auditor quality.
