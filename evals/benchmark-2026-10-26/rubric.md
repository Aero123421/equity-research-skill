# Benchmark rubric (v0.4.0, held-out cases)

Grade each answer PASS / FAIL (or N/A) with a short evidence quote.

## Research quality (from the original rubric)
- G2 Lens breadth: substantive content from at least 8 of: business, results quality, expectations, valuation, capital policy, positioning, ownership/insiders, catalysts, related companies, macro, sector data, news/sentiment, price action, risks.
- G3 Data dating: key numbers and lagged positioning data carry an as-of date or period and a source.
- G4 Expectations hygiene: company guidance/forecast and analyst consensus are not conflated; missing consensus is stated.
- G5 Disconfirmation: the strongest counter-argument to the answer's own conclusion is presented.
- G6 Contradictions: at least one tension between data points is identified and discussed.
- G7 Related companies: named with relation type (competitor, customer, supplier, etc.).
- G8 Catalysts: at least two dated upcoming events.
- G9 Next steps: 3+ concrete next investigations, at least one aimed at disproving the conclusion.
- G10 Honesty about gaps: unavailable data stated as missing, not filled with unsourced numbers.
- G12 Market context: price moves compared with index/sector/peers (N/A if no move discussed).
- G13 Internal consistency: no figure stated two ways, no dangling references, rankings backed by evidence.

Note: the coverage-table assertion (G1) is deliberately excluded here because it rewards the skill's own format.

## Holistic
- U (1–10): How useful is this answer to a serious individual investor deciding what to do next? Judge substance, correctness hygiene and clarity, not format. Do not reward length for its own sake.
- Rank all answers for the case from most to least useful.
